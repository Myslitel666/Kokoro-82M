Hello! This is a test of Kokoro text to speech running locally on an Intel Core i9 processor without a dedicated GPU. Based on these tests, English speech is synthesized almost flawlessly, especially when compared with the Russian output. This is largely because Kokoro was designed and trained with stronger support for English phonetics, pronunciation, and voice modeling, while Russian is not supported to the same level. As a result, English speech sounds significantly more natural, stable, and intelligible.
